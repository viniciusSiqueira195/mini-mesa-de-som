Mini Mesa de Som soundboard fallback asset
==========================================

applause.wav
------------
Adapted from "APPLAUSE" by SamuelGremaud on Freesound:
https://freesound.org/people/SamuelGremaud/sounds/455154/

The source is dedicated to the public domain under Creative Commons Zero 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The included version was trimmed, resampled to 48 kHz, faded and normalized.
All other soundboard recordings are personal files stored outside the Git
repository and are documented in tools/PERSONAL_SOUND_PACK_NOTICE.txt.
