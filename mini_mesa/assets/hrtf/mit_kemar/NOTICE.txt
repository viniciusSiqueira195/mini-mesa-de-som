HRTF Measurements of a KEMAR Dummy-Head Microphone

Authors: Bill Gardner and Keith Martin
Institution: MIT Media Lab Machine Listening Group
Original publication: 1994
Source: https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR.html

The included compact horizontal-plane HRIR files are derived from the official
MIT KEMAR data archive. The archive states:

"This data is Copyright 1994 by the MIT Media Laboratory. It is provided free
with no restrictions on use, provided the authors are cited when the data is
used in any research or commercial application."

Files included here: H0e000a.wav, H0e045a.wav, H0e090a.wav, H0e135a.wav and
H0e180a.wav. Negative angles are produced by exchanging the left and right ear
responses, and intermediate angles are interpolated at runtime.
